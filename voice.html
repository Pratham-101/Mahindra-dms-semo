Built to be ridden

Everything sharpened, nothing softened

Three things make the RTR 310 feel quicker than the numbers suggest: how early the torque arrives, how little the chassis argues, and how much the electronics stay out of the way until you need them.

Five ride modes

Urban, Rain, Sport, Track and Supermoto — each remapping throttle response, ABS and traction control together rather than one at a time.

0–100 in 7.1s

A reverse-inclined DOHC single that pulls cleanly from 3,000 rpm and keeps going to a 10,000 rpm ceiling.

Cornering safety suite

Lean-sensitive ABS and traction control, with rear-lift protection and a slipper clutch that keeps downshifts tidy.
